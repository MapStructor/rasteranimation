This viewer is built by Geodan Research. It shows the date of construction of buildings in the Netherlands, using the BAG data.

You can use the first slider to adjust the years. The second slider adjusts the background map opacity.

The BAG data is served by EduGIS
The background map is OpenStreetMap data.

For more info:
twitter: @stvno
mail: steven.ottens@geodan.nl

Note: Data may be inaccurate for buildings before 1850; "unknown" years appear black.
